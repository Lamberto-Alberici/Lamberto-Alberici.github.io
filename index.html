I'm a rising senior at DePauw University double majoring in Computer Science and Business Analytics. I work where data meets real decisions, combining statistical modeling and machine learning with business thinking to build things that are technically sound and actually useful.

Most recently, my team placed 3rd nationally in the 2026 NCAA Men's Final Four Analytics Challenge out of 198 teams across 15 universities, building a two-stage XGBoost and CatBoost system to replicate the NCAA selection committee's process with 98% ROC-AUC.

Outside of analytics, I serve as a Resident Assistant and President of the DePauw Volleyball Club, where I led the team to a tournament championship with a rebuilt roster.
